<![CDATA[ <p>I find this to be an interesting concept and executed well. At first Javis Wilford seems like a bomb waiting to go off. Then, the Properties are introduced in a well-staged way to lead the reader directly down the intended path of realizations. (Also, I thought these Properties were a good format screw. The presentation of the Article feels fresh.)</p> <p>The ConProcs indicate that we should think about and discuss this Javis Wilford. Property 1 tells us that we shouldn't know too much about him. Maybe he's a cognitohazard or meme.</p> <p>Anyone would be made anxious by the idea of a figure lurking beyond knowledge that's forbidden from access. Property 2 addresses this. It also tells us he's a regular guy&#8212; at this point, that could still be part of the anomaly, because how could a regular guy blow up the Foundation? P.S. if you can't stop worrying about Javis we have no choice but to kill you (which is both realistic for the Foundation and fuel to the fire).</p> <p>Property 3 is a lighthearted departure. We all know Jarvis. Harmless. P.S. we found a loophole where we're still allowed to kill you.</p> <p>It all comes together so nicely. My favorite interpretation of the Foundation is a black-and-white authoritarian structure that's so inflexible it's brittle, and ends up shooting itself in the foot with its attempts to constrain the anomalous. This Article shows that well: they've gone and panicked themselves about a totally normal guy to the point that they've made it possible for him to destroy them in the first place.</p> ]]>
