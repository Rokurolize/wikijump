Critique

– hide block

Let's talk narrative. Do you want to have a narrative in the first place is the big question. If so, what kind of narrative are we gonna be looking at? Is it character-driven, or will it be about a theme that's complimented by the anomaly's hunger for global assimilation, like horror or something else?

To maintain global secrecy, they keep strict isolation of the anomalous object, and prevent disclosure of its existence.

I feel like this is a given since this is the SCP Foundation. Do you mean protect the global population? Or was that the full intent? I feel like the Foundation should be be more cautious of the anomaly considering its goal and capabilities. Something should be done about that aspect.

The foundation goes through multiple accidents and deadly tests trying to figure out where the object came from, what the object is and why it assimilates life, with the potential threat to end humanity.

Do we as a reader get to find out where it came from or any of the other information regarded above? If not, then what were the answers? These are important details that seem to have been intentionally left out.

<![CDATA[ <p><strong>Seeking Greenlights:</strong> No <em>(interested in brainstorming)</em></p> <p><strong>Page Type:</strong> Tale</p> <p><strong>Elevator Pitch:</strong> A Foundation vessel sinks and is transported to Tharlysion, the afterlife of ships. An anomalous artifact that was aboard lets &quot;her&quot; send messages back to the overworld, so she uses it to document her explorations. The Foundation finds those logs when they move to recover the artifact.</p> <p><strong>Central Narrative:</strong> <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argo">The tale of the Argo follows the original myth</a>, but Poseidon despised the Argo as the first ship ever built. He saw it as an intrusion into his domain by mortals and a lesser object, so he set traps along the voyage for them, which created a grudge.</p> <p>After the journey, Athena gathered Argo's broken wreckage, carrying it to Olympus, asking Zeus to give the ship a form from her soul, making her a deity. The Argo wanted an afterlife for the souls of other ships, but the Olympians already held every other domain except the realm of memory, ruled by the comparatively weaker <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mnemosyne">Mnemosyne, Titaness of memories.</a></p> <p>Hera, still bitter over Zeus's affair with Mnemosyne, unable to punish a being of equal status, offered a deal to Argo: originating as a physical being, she could punish Mnemosyne on Hera's behalf, and in return would receive the realm. This turned it into an endless ocean by the <em>River Mnemosyne.</em> While the Titaness was sealed inside an abstract conceptual prison that caused a &quot;leak&quot; in the Mnemosphere.</p> <p>Tharlysion represents the Mnemosphere <em>(The space of all information humans are capable of <strong>retaining</strong>)</em>, contained within the Noosphere, alongside the Oneirosphere. Ship souls are conceptual entities <a href="https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-5968">capable of using thaumaturgy</a> in relation to the ship's historical standing in human collective consciousness. Since memories are related to the human experience in the physical world, where all concepts affect the physical layer, the Mnemosphere manifests in a semi-physical form from the perspective of concepts hosted inside of it <em>(similar to the narrative-stacking effect)</em></p> <p>Poseidon sent sea monsters just for the sake of spitting the Argo, but that had the unplanned side effect of monsters slipping through the leak and reaching other layers of reality. In particular, <a href="https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3125">one ugly starfish.</a></p> <p>There are also some other trivial things about Tharlysion for the sake of world-building and references, like islands that turn into the memories of the ships that touch them, and mimics made of stone, like the rock Cronus swallowed&#8230; etc</p> <br/>Forum category: <a href="https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/forum/c-89000">Writing Help / Help: Ideas Critique</a><br/>Forum thread: <a href="https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/forum/t-18353762/tale-tharlysion:an-afterlife-for-ships">[TALE] Tharlysion: An afterlife for ships</a> ]]>
